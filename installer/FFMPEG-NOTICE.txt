Simple Converter ships FFmpeg as two separate programs, ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe,
and runs them as external processes. Simple Converter itself is MIT-licensed.

FFmpeg is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License v3
(see LICENSE.txt in this folder). Build details are in README.txt.

  FFmpeg project:  https://ffmpeg.org
  Source code:     https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg (commit noted in README.txt)
  Windows builds:  https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

You may replace these two files with any other FFmpeg build.
